# Sandbox

The controls around the sandbox canvas on a component's page.

**SandboxToolbar** holds **Popout**s (each a menu of **PopoutOption**s) for the canvas background, viewport width and zoom, and **ToggleButton**s for the outline, measure and right-to-left effects. **BgSwatch** draws the background colours, and **ToolbarValue** shows a popout's current viewport or zoom. **SandboxCanvas** holds the canvas between the toolbar and the drawer. **ParamsForm** is the resizable drawer of **FormRow**s that sets the component's parameters.

These are internal components: their parameters and markup may change without a deprecation period.
