# AI Assistance Disclosure

This project was built with extensive use of AI coding assistants (primarily Gemini and Claude models).

## What this means in practice

We are seasoned, senior developers that have been working in web technologies for decades. This
codebase is being actively used on a daily basis by at least two high impact, high visibility projects.

**We stand behind the code.** The core library — components, parameters, registry,
services, views, and tests — has been reviewed, understood, and deliberately chosen by the
maintainers. AI suggestions were accepted only where they were correct and appropriate;
they were edited or rejected where they were not. The test suite exists precisely to catch
cases where they were not.

The gallery front-end uses modern CSS, TS and semantic markup. It has a clean architecture,
good abstractions, and comprehensive tests including for a11y.

**The tooling is first class.** We have AI assisted tooling in place throughout the workflow and we 
believe it means the overall working environment is more resistant to bugs, and more easy for new
contributors to help in, that would otherwise be the case. We have comprehensive tests of various
sorts as well as automated review helpers and other tooling in place that also gives us a 
higher velocity and allows us to respond to tickets quickly.

## What we'd welcome

Contributions are very welcome — whether that is a focused accessibility fix, refactor, a new feature
or a bug fix.
See the [contributing guide](https://github.com/fukalite/dj-design-system/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to get started.

We have been intentionally transparent about this rather than pretending the code is
something it is not. If you spot something that concerns you, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/fukalite/dj-design-system/issues).
